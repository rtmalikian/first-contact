Loss of taste in possible viral infection: etiologies, pathogenesis, and the evidence for treatments

Loss or distortion of taste is a recognised accompaniment of viral infections and was reported very widely during the COVID-19 pandemic. Where most early syntheses pooled smell and taste together and counted frequency only, this review separates them — asking which viruses are associated, what mechanisms have been proposed, and what interventions have actually been studied, with effects, doses and harms.

About / Aims & Scope

First Contact is a monthly, open-access journal publishing evidence synthesis and methods work at the intersection of clinical artificial intelligence, family medicine and primary care. It publishes Cochrane-level systematic reviews and meta-analyses, retrospective analyses of open-source contributions to clinical AI infrastructure, commentaries on primary care evidence and policy, and methods papers on how this research is produced and reviewed.

Every article is published in the open: the full text, data and analysis code are public in the article's GitHub repository, and each article carries a Zenodo DOI. New issues appear monthly; Volume 1 began in October 2026.

Article types

  • Systematic Reviews & Meta-analyses — Cochrane-level syntheses, with protocols, search logs, extraction data and GRADE assessments published alongside.
  • Retrospective Analyses — practitioner-contributor analyses of open-source work in clinical AI, health-data infrastructure and related tooling.
  • Commentaries — evidence-based commentary on primary care, family medicine and public health.
  • Methods Papers — how the research is made: review processes, peer-review systems and reproducibility methods.

Editorial and peer review

Editor: Raphael Malikian. Before publication, every manuscript undergoes a structured multi-agent pre-submission peer review — a panel of persona-based reviewers, including a dedicated skeptic, working through fix-and-re-review loops until no blocker or major issue remains open — under the editor's sole accountability as author and editor. Review reports are published with the article's repository. This is pre-submission review conducted in the open; it is described honestly as such, and no article claims external journal peer review it has not had.
